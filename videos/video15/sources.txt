- Spacesuit basics (white outer layer deflects sunlight, three kinds of threads, -250 F to 250 F, up to 16 layers, gold-coated sun visor, about 300 feet of cooling tubes, regulator keeps pressure, some suits have stripes to tell spacewalkers apart): NASA, "Spacewalk and Spacesuit Basics" https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/johnson/spacewalk-spacesuit-basics/
- Extravehicular Mobility Unit (4.3 psi, PLSS 8 hours plus 30 minutes backup, ISS EVA suit total 319 lb, gloves with heaters for the night part of each 95-minute orbit, SAFER in use since 1998, Snoopy cap): Wikipedia, "Extravehicular Mobility Unit" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extravehicular_Mobility_Unit
- Space suits (A7L layers incl. five aluminized insulation layers and white Ortho-Fabric, pure-oxygen suit pressure 32.4 kPa and shuttle EMU 30 kPa, heat released through radiator on the PLSS, commander's stripes from Apollo 13, consciousness about 15 seconds in vacuum, flight suit cost about US$12 million): Wikipedia, "Space suit" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_suit
